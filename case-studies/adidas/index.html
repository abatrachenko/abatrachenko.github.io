Case study · Adidas

A global blog program built from zero

Twelve months from no content infrastructure to 240,000 daily unique clicks from organic search.

EngagementDurationOrganic outcome
Global blog program, built from scratch 12 months +240K daily unique clicks · ~6M daily impressions

Outcome reflects organic visibility gains in the engagement’s own unit.

Context

Adidas had no existing blog infrastructure and no organic footprint for editorial content. The opportunity was clear at enterprise scale — but the asset, the org buy-in, and the operating model all had to be created before any SEO work could compound.

The work

This engagement covered the full arc, embedded inside the Adidas organization:

  • The case: engineered the pitch and sold the concept to senior leadership — securing funding and dedicated development resources before a single page existed.
  • The build: technical architecture and requirements for a global blog platform, created from zero.
  • The content engine: content strategy plus hands-on execution — twelve months of sustained work across the interlock of engineering, content, and marketing that enterprise SEO requires.
  • Go-to-market: led the launch and the growth phase that followed.

Result

The blog now generates roughly 6 million impressions and 240,000 unique clicks a day from organic search — an asset that did not exist when the engagement began.

“Aleksey manifested the adidas Blog from scratch. He engineered the pitch, sold the concept to senior leadership, secured funding and dev resources, created the requirements, and led GTM. Now the blog generates ~6 million impressions and 240K unique clicks a day. The long-term value Aleksey created for adidas is extraordinary.”
SVP Digital adidas Group
