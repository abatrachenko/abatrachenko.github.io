Case study · J.Crew

Long-tail category architecture

Nine months of category page architecture that unlocked demand the team didn’t know existed: +95% organic clicks.

EngagementDurationOrganic outcome
Long-tail category page architecture 9 months +95% organic clicks

Outcome reflects organic visibility gains in the engagement’s own unit.

Context

J.Crew’s category structure served the demand the team already knew about. The long tail — the specific, lower-volume queries that add up to a major channel at enterprise scale — had no landing surface designed for organic discovery.

The work

The engagement designed a long-tail category page architecture: landing pages structured around how buyers actually search, built to be discovered organically rather than navigated to. The approach followed the standard embedded model — a phased roadmap prioritized by impact, integrated with the teams already running the site.

Result

Organic clicks grew 95% over the nine-month engagement — traffic from demand that had been invisible to the previous category structure.

“Aleksey’s work on J.Crew’s long-tail SEO strategy was transformative. His approach to category landing pages unlocked traffic we didn’t even know existed. The 95% growth in organic clicks speaks for itself.”
VP Marketing J.Crew
