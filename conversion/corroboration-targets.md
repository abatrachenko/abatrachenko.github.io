## 90-day sequence (1–2 placements/month, ~4–6 hrs/week)

**Month 1 — free compounding infrastructure + the open door:**
1. HARO + Qwoted + SOS signups (day 1; then ~30 min/day triage; enterprise-SEO/GEO/e-comm queries only). Only channel that can land a placement within weeks at DR 0.
2. **SEL contributor application** — the open call matches the credential bar exactly; one SEL byline outranks everything else here for citation probability. Angle: GEO-for-enterprise (Reach LLM advisory = defensible dataset).
3. Reddit presence (r/TechSEO, r/bigseo) under a consistent identity — free and unblockable.

**Month 2 — voice placements + directory hygiene:**
4. Two low-effort podcast pitches: a David Bain multi-guest format (near-guaranteed) + EDGE of the Web or Voices of Search. Pitch the enterprise-brand story + GEO, not "SEO tips."
5. Free directory profiles in one sitting: Semrush directory, Clutch (+start collecting 2–3 verified client reviews), the new AI-native lists. No paid placements anywhere.

**Month 3 — high-leverage asks, now with proof points:**
6. SEJ contributor pitch (enterprise-GEO niche), citing the month 1–2 footprint.
7. Roundup outreach: Siege Media (ahead of the Jan refresh — contact Nov/Dec) + First Page Sage (their methodology scores exactly what months 1–2 built).
8. Speaking pipeline: submit BrightonSEO Apr-2027 + SMX rolling pitches now (forms cost an hour; lead times demand it); calendar-watch MozCon's next window.

**Ordering logic:** quote services + Reddit produce citations at DR 0 with zero gatekeeping → SEL's open call is time-limited and highest-authority → podcasts create bio pages + transcripts on aged domains that roundup editors then find → roundup outreach converts only once the earlier layers exist to point at.

**Monthly measurement:** run "best enterprise SEO consultant" / "enterprise GEO consultant"-class prompts across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini/AI Overviews, log which roundups get cited (turns list #3 from inference into a measured target set) and whether Aleksey appears.
