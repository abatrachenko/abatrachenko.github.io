## Decisions (derived, per plan)

- **Backend: Buttondown.** Newsletter-native (collects AND sends the every-other-Tuesday emails — Tally/Formspree only collect), plain-HTML `<form>` POST embed, zero third-party JS → no perf/BP cost. Owner creates the account; PR ships as **draft** with `BUTTONDOWN_USERNAME` placeholder until the real username is supplied. A dead form never reaches `main`.
- **No inline Calendly embed in P1.** Current links already give instant scheduling; the widget's payload risks perf 99/BP gates for marginal gain. Revisit on the P2 `/contact` page.
- **Placement:** (1) unnumbered capture band between References (04) and Availability (05) — no `id`, so the verify-site `main section[id]` order assertion is untouched and the eyebrow numbering stays intact; (2) compact variant in the footer. Two placements, capture stays visually subordinate to the call CTAs (call-primary model).
- **Voice:** locked positioning wording as the headline; audit-endorsed value line as support. Both need owner sign-off:
  - H2: "Organic growth patterns for enterprise e-commerce"
  - Support: "What's actually moving organic and AI search — written from inside the engagements. Every other Tuesday."
  - Button: "Subscribe" · microcopy: "Unsubscribe anytime."
- **Pricing rationale (featured 48h row):** "Where most enterprise teams land — enough hours to own the roadmap, not just advise on it." Scope-neutral (no invented deliverables); owner approves.
- **Analytics:** `newsletter_signup` GA4 event on submit with `location` param (midpage/footer), gtag-guarded like `book_call_click`.
- **A11y:** visible-on-focus labels via sr-only + placeholder, 44px input/button height, focus-visible outlines, no new animation.
