Service · Enterprise SEO

Fractional SEO leadership, embedded in your team

A senior operator working inside your tools, channels, and cadences — not an agency submitting tickets through a portal.

The model

Enterprise SEO engagements at agencies typically pass through account managers to junior execution teams. Here, the senior strategist who scopes the work is the same person executing it. No handoffs, no learning curve on your account, no dilution of strategic judgment through layers.

Embedded means exactly that: working within your team’s tools, channels, and cadences — attending relevant standups, with access to your analytics and CMS, operating as a senior member of your marketing team.

Who it’s for

Enterprise e-commerce brands — the playbook and the pricing are built for organizations at that scale, with 10,000+ URL inventories and the interlock of engineering, content, and marketing that enterprise SEO requires. It is not the right fit for brands under $50M in revenue; smaller brands are better served by different operators.

How engagements run

  • Vetting & discovery. Not every engagement is the right fit. Discovery assesses your business model, technical foundation, competitive landscape, and team dynamics — ending in an honest go/no-go based on real revenue potential.
  • Integration & planning. A phased roadmap prioritized by impact, built around your workflows — JIRA sprints, Slack channels, weekly standups, whatever your teams already run.
  • Implementation & maintenance. Execution oversight from start to finish: writing tickets, QA-ing work, monitoring performance, course-correcting in real time.

Engagement terms

$200 per hour, in monthly blocks of 32, 48, or 64 hours ($6,400 / $9,600 / $12,800). Three-month minimum — organic growth compounds, and shorter engagements don’t give the work room to deliver measurable results. Most engagements run 6–12 months. Full tiers and current availability are on the pricing section.

Scope is SEO and AI search (GEO) only — no paid media, no brand strategy, no generalist growth marketing. AI-search readiness is woven into every engagement, not sold as a separate service.

Proof

$700M+ in organic revenue influenced across 35+ enterprise brands over fifteen years. Three engagements with named outcomes: Adidas (+240K daily clicks), J.Crew (+95% organic clicks), and Alchemy (+315% organic clicks).

Discovery starts with a strategy call — and an honest go/no-go if the fit isn’t there.
