Capability · GEO + AI search

AI-search readiness, woven into the engagement

Classical SEO execution and GEO strategy run by the same senior operator — not a separate vendor, not a bolt-on service with its own price tag.

What GEO means here

Generative Engine Optimization is the work of making a brand visible and citable in AI-powered search — Google’s AI surfaces, ChatGPT, Perplexity. It is a natural extension of senior-level SEO strategy, and few SEO operators work at senior level across both traditional search and AI-powered search.

What the work covers

  • Content structure — formatting and organizing content so AI systems can retrieve and cite it.
  • Entity authority — making the brand a well-defined, corroborated entity that AI systems recognize.
  • Citation positioning — earning the placements AI answers actually draw from.

How it’s priced

It isn’t — separately. GEO is integrated into whatever an engagement needs, inside the regular hourly allocation. If a client wants AI-search capability built out, it happens within the same engagement model as everything else. Tooling recommendations depend on the client’s stack, not on vendor alignment.

Why one operator for both

SEO and GEO share the same underlying system: how your content is structured, how your entities are understood, and what the rest of the web says about you. Splitting them across vendors splits strategic judgment across a boundary the search engines don’t recognize. One senior operator owning both means AI-search readiness isn’t a separate roadmap competing for resources — it’s a dimension of every decision already being made.

How visible is your brand in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google’s AI results? That’s a discovery question.
